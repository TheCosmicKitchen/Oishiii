# Oishii おいしい

A cute Japanese learning game for two adult beginners (a couple). One plays the **Kana speller** (romaji helper, reading choices), the other the **Kanji reader** (WaniKani ~level 10, typed readings, bare kanji). Read `STYLE_GUIDE.md` before any change; keep the look consistent across updates.

## Non-negotiable design rules
- **Completion and collection, never engagement.** No streaks, no decay or wilting, no expiring content, no "come back tomorrow" pressure. Everything is permanent and collectable (station stamps, kanji stamps, trophies, stars, archive). Grind is always available.
- **Very cute.** Fluent Emoji 3D art (embedded as WebP data URIs in `assets/emoji.json`), soft spring palette, bouncy animations. Stamps carry no emoji.
- **Show romaji alongside kana** for the kana role. Typing converts romaji to kana like WaniKani (WanaKana).
- **Start from kana; quiz only what's been learned/stamped.**
- **Players study kanji in English.** Never show a kanji they haven't stamped, except the kanji a stop is teaching on that stop's own screens. Show everything else in English with romaji (`canRead` / `placeName` in `src/rail.js`). Station stamps are the one exception: they're collectible art.
- **Learning is not a garden.** Planting and watering kana as the way you learn tested poorly and was removed; don't bring that back. The **Garden tab** is a cozy decorating space instead: stamps are traded in for seeds (each stamp pays once and stays in the book; kanji 1, station 3, city +5), seeds buy plants and decor, watering grows plants a stage, and friends move in on their own. Nothing wilts or decays. Harvesting takes a plant back one stage into the pantry; the kitchen's recipe book and Garden words each pay seeds once per new entry (`bonus` seeds), so nothing can be farmed forever. The sky's time of day and season are scenery only, never a reason to come back. Old saves gain new garden fields through `G()`, which only fills in what's missing.
- Progress is saved per device (localStorage). That's intended.

## Structure
- Tabs: Today (next steps, reviews, JLPT N5 readiness), Kanji (the line), Grammar (the road), Play (Sushi Belt and puzzles), Garden. Collection and settings open from the header.
- Learning kanji is **the line**: a rail trip of 228 real stations (`tools/route.tsv`). Each station is a sub-level: 6 kana stops (Tokyo), then all 2,211 JLPT kanji N5 → N1 in sub-levels of 10. Clearing one lays track on the map and earns a station stamp; its kanji get kanji stamps.
- Garden (`src/garden.js`): shop items unlock as more seeds are traded in; kanji names show only once every kanji in them is stamped (`canRead`), otherwise English, always with kana and romaji. Friends and Garden trophies are permanent.
- Games: Transfer Chain (link two-kanji words from JMdict, `src/compound-data.js`), Radical Builder, Meaning Match, Sushi Belt, Petal Guess, Garden Plot, Garden Logic, Missing Half, Number Line. Each has dailies (same seed for both players), endless mode and 10 levels with stars. Stamps are only station stamps (incl. city stamps) and kanji stamps; puzzles earn stars and trophies, not stamps.
- 36 stops are **cities** (`city` column in `route.tsv`): scalloped city stamps, square map markers, an arrival card and a City stamps page.
- N5/N4 kanji are **grounded**: each stop teaches kanji from its name, its local words (`tools/local_words.tsv`) or station signs within 25 km, plus one local special (名物) per city (`tools/meibutsu.tsv`). Regenerate `src/ground-data.js` with `tools/ground_kanji.py`. Local words must be real and verifiable.
- Towns are being **curated one by one** (starting with Yokohama): `tools/towns.tsv` hand-picks a stop's kanji and the real local word anchoring each, and `src/scenes.js` holds its little scenes (signs, a short exchange, one choice, a note and a real fact). Scenes are told in English; the only Japanese in them is the kanji that stop teaches. A scene can carry a real photo as a postcard: free Wikimedia Commons files only, approved by the user on a photo board, with author and licence in `tools/photos.tsv` (`tools/make_photo_data.py` builds `src/photo-data.js`).
- **The goal is the JLPT.** The players are studying for the N5. Kanji and words follow Waller's JLPT lists; grammar follows the usual N5 points, and drills follow the exam's question types (choose the grammar form, reading). Don't add a drill whose answer can be ambiguous (e.g. sentence ordering on real sentences).
- **Grammar is the road**: a walk along the old Tōkaidō's post towns (`tools/grammar_points.json`), not gated by the kanji line. Each stop has grammar cards, a drill and reading practice. `tools/grammar_build.py` picks native Tatoeba sentences for each point (native writer, native English translation, human furigana that the parser agrees with, N5/N4 words, only grammar already taught) and writes `src/grammar-data.js` and `tools/grammar_report.txt`, which must be read through after any change. Chapter 1 is Nihonbashi to Hakone (27 N5 points, polite style); chapter 3 is Okabe to Maisaka (20 points: と/や/か lists, だけ, ぐらい/ごろ, なる/にする, もう/まだ, あまり/全然, 何か/何も, に行く, たことがある, たり, ながら, 後で, でしょう, とき, verbs describing nouns, なくてもいい); chapter 2 is Mishima to Mariko (20 points: たい, ている, time に, から/まで, plain forms, だ/だった, から and が joining sentences, ないでください, なければならない, てから, 前に, より/ほう, 一番). Each stop has a "ch" (chapter) in grammar_points.json.
- Hidden "secret ticket": tap the train on the map 5× to unlock up to any stop (stamps still earned).

## Working on it
- Edit `src/`, then `python3 build.py`. It writes `index.html` (served by GitHub Pages at https://thecosmickitchen.github.io/Oishiii/) and `dist/oishii.html` (the Claude artifact version).
- `src/kanji-data.js`, `src/deck-data.js`, `src/compound-data.js`, `src/route-data.js` and `src/ground-data.js` are generated; regenerate with the scripts in `tools/` (see README). Don't edit them by hand.
- **The user is a learner and can't check Japanese.** Every reading, meaning, word or sentence must come from an openly licensed, well-known source (KANJIDIC2, JMdict, JmdictFurigana, Waller's lists, Tatoeba) and pass at least two independent checks before it ships. Don't use WaniKani data (its content belongs to WaniKani) or small unvetted datasets. Judgment calls go in a reviewed file with a reason (e.g. `tools/kanji_primary_review.tsv`). Main readings come from `tools/kanji_deck.py`, never hand edits.
- Facts on station cards must be real and verifiable; keep them to one short line.
- Keep the README credits for KANJIDIC2/RADKFILE/JMdict (EDRDG, CC BY-SA 4.0), Kanji alive, Tanos JLPT lists, ekidata, Global Map Japan and every town photo.
- Test with a headless browser (Playwright is available) at 400px wide in light and dark before pushing.
